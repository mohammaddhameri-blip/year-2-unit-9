Today's Goals

Quick Flash

Pupils say the object name aloud. Click the picture to show the next object.

Choose an object from the bottom, then click the sea, sand, bucket or sandcastle to place it. Keep clicking new spots to move that object. Reselect the same picture to add another copy.

Selected: none • Now click where you want the object to go.

Examples: put the shell on the sandcastle, put the ball in the bucket, or click any exact spot in the sea or sand. Then ask: “Where's the ___?”
